# `quant-market-research` boundary

Language: English · [简体中文](market-research-boundary.md)

`quant-market-research` owns project-specific market evidence, including long-term style-factor results and six-market ETF proxy-allocation studies.

`quant-platform` owns reusable backtesting, portfolio ledgers, risk, cost, execution simulation, and research-artifact contracts. Study windows, target market weights, ETF proxy choices, and factor-promotion rules belong to the research repository.

The style-factor code excerpt from `portfolio-backtester` is not included in the public release because its source license has not been confirmed. Do not move that material into `quant-market-research` as a workaround.
